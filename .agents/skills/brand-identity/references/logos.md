# Logos

---

## History — Three Peaks

Our history is rooted in the outdoors and draws inspiration from the great Couloirs of our time. Chutes which lead to new adventures and new stories worth telling.

The three peaks for ABK Labs are: **Corbits**, **Faremeter**, **Interchange**.

Our brand system reflects them by leveraging orange as primary and accent colors while maintaining clear differentiating light and dark contrasting colors for commercial and open-source work respectively.

## Design Rules

1. **Commercial vs. open-source separation:** Corbits and Interchange use orange (commercial); Faremeter uses black and white (open-source, neutral).
2. **Font separation:** Corbits uses Belwe (serif); Faremeter and Interchange share TratexSvart (display) but differ in color.
3. **The IX monogram** uses extreme negative letter-spacing to create a distinctive overlapping mark; the FM monogram has normal spacing.
