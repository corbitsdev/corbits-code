# Illustration

---

## 01 illustration/painting

### Do

- Seek out Werner Bronkhorst for inspiration
- Find mini subjects that are fun and playful
- Embrace tranquility through motion (still boats)

### Don't

- Be overcome by giant brush strokes
- Find too much detail in detail
- Get too focused on one brilliant subject

### Artistic Reference

Werner Bronkhorst — contemporary South African painter known for thick impasto oil paintings of coastal/beach scenes with tiny human figures. Bold, heavy brush strokes in vibrant colors (greens, blues, teals) with miniature people surfing, swimming, or walking on beaches.

## 02 illustration/magazines

### Do

- Find inspiration in an old sport magazine
- Say something bold
- Seek adrenaline with the scenery

### Don't

- Forget to include space for language
- Overlook a compelling subject
- Be afraid to mix and match

## General Direction

**Painting:** Heavy impasto oil painting in the Werner Bronkhorst style. Tiny, playful human or boat subjects against vast painted landscapes. The paint texture itself is a major visual element but should not overwhelm the composition.

**Magazines:** Vintage/retro adventure sport magazine aesthetic. Bold typography paired with dramatic action photography. Collage-style compositions that mix text, photography, and illustration. Space for language/typography is essential. The mood is adventurous, bold, and slightly irreverent.
