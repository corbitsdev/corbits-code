# Statements — Category Hierarchy

External positioning taxonomy defining where Corbits sits in the market.

## External Category

**NEOTEAM**

Modern teams composed of humans and agents.

## External Subcategory

**AGENT OPS** (Agent Operations)

Modern business units dedicated to understanding, implementing, and securing AI in the workplace.

## External Focus

**INTEROP**

The ability of different systems, software, devices, or organizations to work together.

---

## Hierarchy

```
NEOTEAM (category)
  └── AGENT OPS (subcategory)
        └── INTEROP (focus)
```

## Usage

- **Category** — broadest framing; used in analyst briefings, market maps, investor materials
- **Subcategory** — operational framing; used in product positioning, landing pages, sales decks
- **Focus** — technical differentiator; used in feature messaging, developer content, technical comparisons
