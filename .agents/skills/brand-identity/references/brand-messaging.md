# Brand Messaging

Elevator pitches, core messages, and proof points. Use when writing about
Corbits at any level of detail -- from a seven-word answer at a dinner party
to a full capabilities overview.

## One-Liner

What you say when someone asks "What does your company do?" Seven to twelve
words. No jargon.

**Full form:** We build Agent Operations for neoteams.

**Abbreviated:** We build AgentOps for neoteams.

Use the abbreviated form in casual or space-constrained contexts (tweets,
bios, button labels). Use the full form in formal contexts (press releases,
partner pages, investor materials).

---

## Elevator Pitches

Thirty seconds. Three to four sentences. Who you serve, what you do, why it
matters, and what makes you different. Use for website headlines, LinkedIn
summaries, capabilities decks.

Three variants exist. Choose based on audience and emphasis.

### Variant 1 — Scale and Control

> When teams want to work with agents, they use us. At Corbits we build
> AgentOps for Businesses that need to scale. Without our platform neoteams
> are deployed without organization, permissions, or real controls which can
> lead to databases being erased or millions lost in faulty contracts. Our
> platforms are designed with interoperability and multi-tenancy as first class
> features with zero vendor-lock.

**Best for:** Technical buyers, platform evaluators, security-conscious orgs.
Emphasizes interoperability and zero vendor lock-in.

### Variant 2 — Leverage and Growth

> When teams don't know how to use agents, they call us. At Corbits we help
> Businesses that need to leverage AI to scale. Without our platform neoteams
> are created without organization, permissions, or real controls which can
> lead to databases being erased or millions lost in faulty contracts. Our
> platforms give neoteams the peace of mind they can growth without needing to
> make costly commitments today that won't work tomorrow.

**Best for:** Non-technical decision makers, executives evaluating AI
adoption. Emphasizes peace of mind and future-proofing.

### Variant 3 — Multiplayer and Collaboration

> When neoteams want to work together, they use us. At Corbits we build agent
> platforms with real interoperability. Without our platform neoteams are
> isolated without organization, tooling, or real controls which prevent them
> from working fast and free. Our platforms are designed with multiplayer and
> agent2agent as first class features with zero vendor-lock.

**Best for:** Companies already running agents who need cross-org
coordination. Emphasizes multiplayer and agent-to-agent capability.

## Core Messages

Five pillars that define what Corbits stands for. Use these as the backbone
of any long-form content, pitch deck, or campaign narrative.

### 01 — Dogfooding

We use our own products. Everyone on our team is capable of shipping new
features and improvements and that extends to our customers regardless of
size.

**When to use:** Credibility moments. Differentiation from competitors who
build tools they don't use. Customer success stories.

### 02 — Scale

Scale matters. Maybe not today, but for someone, and soon. At Corbits we
build systems that are meant to be deployed for teams who dream big. That
means anyone should be able to start with our systems and keep using them
till the job is truly finished.

**When to use:** Enterprise conversations. Growth-stage companies. Anyone
worried about outgrowing their current tools.

### 03 — Communication

Communication is a core tenet of everything we do. It's how neoteams work.
It's how our customers speak to us and how we speak with our customers.

**When to use:** Product messaging around agent coordination. Internal
culture storytelling. Customer support positioning.

### 04 — Control

The promise is complete control for when the 0.00001% thing does happens. We
would love to believe nothing will ever go wrong, but we design our systems
to ensure you have all the resources and information at your disposal to
solve anything.

**When to use:** Compliance and governance conversations. Risk-averse
buyers. Audit and provenance messaging.

### 05 — Mission

Our Mission is Mars. We've put software on the quadcopter. We want to do it
again.

**When to use:** Vision and culture content. Recruiting. Investor
materials. Moments where aspiration matters more than features.

## Proof Points

Evidence that backs up the core messages. Use when claims need teeth.

### Dogfooding Proof

We built our team by dogfooding our tools. We're a 5 person start-up with
$4M in funding with team members constantly pulled in every direction from
engineering to customer support to marketing. Our competitors and industry
are the who's who of startups and frontier labs with AMEX Black Cards for
token/compute while we have debit cards.

**When to use:** Underdog narrative. Credibility under resource constraints.
"How do you compete with well-funded incumbents?" conversations.
