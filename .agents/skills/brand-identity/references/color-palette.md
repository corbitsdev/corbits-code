# Color Palette

---

## 01 color/primary

Breakthrough Orange #E98428 is your action color — warm, energetic, the sunrise after a long climb. Use it for primary CTAs and moments that demand attention. Bedrock Charcoal #2B2627 carries the weight: typography, structure, the bones of the interface. Summit Blue #607C9A and Ridge Green #7B9974 balance the warmth with cool information and success signals. Canvas Cream #F7EAD5 is the ground — backgrounds, breathing room, the space where work happens.

| Name | Hex | CSS Variable | Role |
|------|-----|-------------|------|
| Canvas Cream | `#F7EAD5` | `--color/primary/canvas-cream` | Backgrounds, breathing room (~60%) |
| Breakthrough Orange | `#E98428` | `--color/primary/breakthrough-orange` | Action color, primary CTAs, attention moments |
| Bedrock Charcoal | `#2B2627` | `--color/primary/bedrock-charcoal` | Typography, structure, interface bones (~25%) |
| Summit Blue | `#607C9A` | `--color/primary/summit-blue` | Cool information, secondary signals |
| Ridge Green | `#7B9974` | `--color/primary/ridge-green` | Success, completion signals |

---

## 02 color/dark

Dark mode isn't an inversion, it's a different time of day. Bedrock Charcoal #1F1A1B becomes the vast night sky — dominant ground, never pure black. Breakthrough Orange #BF6B20 glows warmer and demands even more restraint — save it for the one action that matters. Canvas Cream #E4D5BC flips to light text and borders, moonlight rather than clinical white. Keep contrast high and colors minimal; dark mode rewards restraint. Summit Blue #2D455C takes on dark tones and Ridge Green #425A3D grows dark.

| Name | Hex | CSS Variable |
|------|-----|-------------|
| Canvas Cream | `#E4D5BC` | `--color/dark/canvas-cream` |
| Breakthrough Orange | `#BF6B20` | `--color/dark/breakthrough-orange` |
| Bedrock Charcoal | `#1F1A1B` | `--color/dark/bedrock-charcoal` |
| Summit Blue | `#2D455C` | `--color/dark/summit-blue` |
| Ridge Green | `#425A3D` | `--color/dark/ridge-green` |

---

## 03 color/light

Canvas Cream #FFFFFF is your canvas — 60% of any composition, the space that shapes the work. Bedrock Charcoal #5C5555 carries all text and structure at roughly 25%. Summit Blue #C5D2DE handles secondary information; Ridge Green #C1D1BE signals success and completion. Breakthrough Orange #F2B277 maintains power through scarcity — primary buttons only, once per screen. Never more than three colors in a single view.

| Name | Hex | CSS Variable |
|------|-----|-------------|
| Canvas Cream | `#FFFFFF` | `--color/light/canvas-cream` |
| Breakthrough Orange | `#F2B277` | `--color/light/breakthrough-orange` |
| Bedrock Charcoal | `#5C5555` | `--color/light/bedrock-charcoal` |
| Summit Blue | `#C5D2DE` | `--color/light/summit-blue` |
| Ridge Green | `#C1D1BE` | `--color/light/ridge-green` |
