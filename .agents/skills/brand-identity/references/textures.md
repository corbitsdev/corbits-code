## Usage Guidelines

- The 322XL texture at 23% opacity creates the subtlest effect — barely visible brush strokes over a warm, almost solid orange field.
- The 325XL texture produces a dark, moody result — suitable for dark-mode hero sections.
- The 328XL texture shows large, sweeping, directional brush strokes — sculptural and three-dimensional with clear light/shadow on paint ridges.
- Base color is always Breakthrough Orange `#E98428`.
- No imagery, no figures, no landscape elements — pure paint texture.
