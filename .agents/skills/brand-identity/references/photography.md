# Photography

---

## 01 photography/photos

### Do

- Identify interesting subjects
- Find sequences or patterns that are memorable or contrasting
- Settle every once in a while on expansive backgrounds

### Don't

- Fall in love with one subject and miss the action
- Think too little
- Think too much

## 02 photography/sports

### Do

- Use overhead shots
- Find images where the subject is 10-15% of the image
- Seek out still shots that are of action activities

### Don't

- Choose busy images with many different focal points
- Settle for subjects or backgrounds that do not invoke action
- Not have a subject, no matter how small

## General Direction

Favor images where the subject is relatively small against a larger, often expansive backdrop. Look for visual contrast and memorable compositions. Sports photography strongly favors aerial/overhead/bird's-eye perspectives with subjects at 10-15% of frame. Clean compositions with a single clear focal area, not cluttered.
