# Products — Ecosystem Overview

The Corbits product ecosystem. Use this reference when explaining, positioning, or writing about any product.

---

## ABK Labs

The legal entity behind the Corbits product line. ABK Labs is the company; Corbits is the trade name.

- **Legal name:** ABK Labs
- **Trade name:** Corbits
- **Refer to as:** "we" (never "it")

---

## 01 Corbits

**What it is:** The company and commercial platform brand. The premiere hosted platform for neoteams. AgentOps for neoteams — the business units for organizations that need to govern, orchestrate, and scale teams of humans and agents.

**What it does:** Provides the infrastructure for neoteams to operate with organization, permissions, controls, and interoperability. Designed for multi-tenancy and zero vendor lock-in.

**Who it's for:** Businesses from pre-seed to public that are building neoteams — modern teams composed of humans and agents. Target buyers include AI Directors, Heads of AI, AI Consultants, AI Task Force Leads, and Chief Agent Officers.

**One-liner:** "We build Agent Operations for neoteams."

**Market position:**
- Category: Neoteam
- Subcategory: Agent Ops (Agent Operations)
- Focus: Interop

**Brand notes:** Lowercase wordmark ("corbits") in design assets, but always capitalize as "Corbits" in running text. Breakthrough Orange. Belwe Bd BT Bold. Approachable, pragmatic, confident, collaborative, and human.

## 02 Interchange

**What it is:** The orchestration layer that coordinates agents across companies. AgentOps for neoteams — the software for businesses that need to govern, orchestrate, and scale teams of humans and agents.

**What it does:** Coordinates handoffs between agents without centralized control. Enables multiplayer agent-to-agent communication, federation, provenance tracking, and org-wide governance.

**Who it's for:** Teams deploying agent fleets that need orchestration, compliance, and cross-organizational communication. From solo founders who need multiplayer capabilities to enterprises upgrading legacy systems.

**Value it creates:**
1. Federate agent teams (reduce headcount)
2. Track provenance (reduce compliance costs)
3. Inter-organization communication (improve customer success)
4. Org-wide governance (reduce risk, accelerate deployment)

**Brand notes:** ALL CAPS wordmark ("INTERCHANGE"). Breakthrough Orange. TratexSvart. "IX" monogram with tight negative spacing. Forward momentum.

---

## 03 Faremeter

**What it is:** A universal framework designed to allow agents and other utilities to transparently make machine payments using web infrastructure.

**What it does:** Settles payments and records provenance. Provides agent wallets and multi-protocol support.

**Who it's for:** Developers and platforms building machine-to-machine payment flows. Open-source contributors. Anyone who needs payment settlement between autonomous systems.

**Relationship to Corbits:** Independent open-source project. Corbits contributes to Faremeter; Interchange uses it to power machine payments. Faremeter belongs to the ecosystem, not to a company.

**Brand notes:** ALL CAPS wordmark ("FAREMETER"). Pure Black. TratexSvart. "FM" monogram. Black and white only — reflects how unopinionated it is. Faremeter never sounds corporate.

## Product Relationships

```
ABK Labs (legal entity)
└── Corbits (trade name / company brand)
    ├── Interchange (hosted platform — commercial)
    └── contributes to Faremeter (open-source)
```

**Commercial vs. open-source:** Corbits and Interchange use orange (commercial). Faremeter uses black and white (open-source, neutral). This separation is reflected across logos, wordmarks, and color usage.

**When writing about products:**
- Interchange is the product buyers purchase. Lead with Interchange when talking to customers.
- Faremeter is the ecosystem project. Lead with Faremeter when talking to developers and open-source communities.
- Corbits is the company. Lead with Corbits when talking about the team, mission, or company story.
- Never blend the commercial and open-source voices in a single piece.
